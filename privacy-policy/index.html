Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026. This policy explains what information we handle when you use proplumbinglenexa.com or call our line.

Call (855) 575-2890Read the FAQ

Calls and call recording

Our service is call-only. When you call, we collect the phone number you call from, your service address and details about your plumbing problem so a plumber can help. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

How we use information

We use call information to dispatch a plumber, give you a price, follow up on a job and improve our service. We do not use web forms on this site.

Website data

Like most sites, our server logs may record basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited. We do not use this data to identify you personally. Embedded Google Maps on our pages may set their own cookies under Google's policies.

Sharing

We share your name, address and problem description with the plumber assigned to your job and with service providers who help us run the line. We may disclose information when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the information we hold about a call by emailing contact@proplumbinglenexa.com.
