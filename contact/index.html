What to tell dispatch

  1. What is happening: leak, backup, no hot water, gas smell, or a planned job.
  2. Where in the house it is, and whether water is on the floor now.
  3. Whether you have shut off the main water valve yet.
  4. Your street address and the best number to reach you.
