Lenexa and its ZIP codes

Lenexa is a Johnson County city of 34.5 square miles. We serve the ZIP codes 66215, 66219, 66220 and 66227, along with the neighborhoods around Lenexa City Center, Sar-Ko-Par Trails Park, Shawnee Mission Park and the Prairie Star Parkway growth area. Neighborhood names such as Canyon Creek, Cottonwood Canyon, Whispering Hills, Century Estates and Bristol Ridge are all part of the territory.

For emergency work inside the city, start at our emergency drain cleaning page for backups, or the burst pipe repair page for water you cannot stop.

Overland Park, the largest neighbor

Overland Park shares Lenexa's eastern edge and is the biggest city we serve outside Lenexa. We have a full page for it, covering ZIP codes, local water and sewer contacts, and what homeowners there call about most: emergency plumber in Overland Park.

Shawnee, Olathe, De Soto, Mission and Leawood

Shawnee sits to the north of Lenexa and Olathe to the south. De Soto lies farther west, and Mission and Leawood are on the east and southeast side. Each is in the same dispatch pattern, and crews head out from Lenexa for emergencies such as a sewer line repair or a failed tank that needs a water heater replacement.

These towns have their own city permit offices. Before any permit-based work outside Lenexa, check with that city's building department.

How dispatch decides who goes where

Every call goes to one 24/7 line, and dispatch routes the nearest available crew. A crew finishing a job in Lenexa may be closer to a Shawnee address than one coming from across town, so the map matters less than who is free.

That is why we give an arrival window and not a promise. Storms, closures and a busy night change the time, and dispatch will tell you honestly if a delay is likely.

Water and sewer service across the area

WaterOne supplies drinking water to most of Johnson County, including Lenexa, and publishes an annual water quality report. Its Service Line Lookup Tool shows the service line material at an address, and WaterOne states that it has no record of lead service lines in its distribution system.

Johnson County Wastewater serves the public sewer and can be reached at 913-715-8600. Its voluntary JoCo Inflow program has inspected more than 55,000 properties since 1983 and removed about 17,000 stormwater sources, and it can be reached at 913-715-8550.

Permits change from city to city

Lenexa permits come from Lenexa Community Development at 913-477-7725, under the 2018 International Plumbing Code (IPC) the City has adopted. Overland Park, Shawnee, Olathe, De Soto, Mission and Leawood each run their own offices, so a rule in one city may not apply in the next.

Before a water heater replacement, a repipe or a sewer repair, ask the city where the house sits what applies. Use a contractor licensed through Johnson County, and check at cls.jocogov.org.

Lenexa neighborhoods and what their pipes tell us

Lenexa was incorporated in 1907 and has grown steadily since. Listing records place areas such as Whispering Hills in the 1970s and 1980s and Bristol Ridge in the 2010s, and Old Town Lenexa is the older center of the city. House age matters because it hints at pipe material and drain layout.

Older homes are more likely to have copper, galvanized steel or cast iron drains, while newer ones typically use PEX and plastic drains. We check what is actually in your walls before we quote, because a repair that suits one street can be wrong for the next.

What to tell dispatch about your location

Give the street address and the nearest cross street. If you are on the edge of Lenexa and Overland Park, say so, since the two cities have different permit offices. Mention any gate codes, pets and the best door to use.

If you are not sure your address is covered, call anyway. Dispatch will confirm and, if we cannot reach you, say so on the call instead of leaving you waiting.

Towns close to the border

Some addresses sit on the line between two cities, and the ZIP code does not always match the city name on a mailing address. A home with a Lenexa mailing address can be in a different city for permit purposes, and the reverse is also true.

Tell dispatch the street and the nearest cross street. If a permit is needed, we help you work out which office applies, and the city offices can confirm the boundary. Shawnee, Olathe, De Soto, Mission and Leawood each have their own permit rules.

Whatever the city, the first step in an emergency is the same: stop the water if you can, keep clear of electrical hazards and call the line.
