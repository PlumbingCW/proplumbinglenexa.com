Plumber in Canyon Creek, Lenexa, KS

Canyon Creek is a cluster of homeowner associations in west Lenexa. The City's HOA list names Canyon Creek, Canyon Ridge, Canyon Creek Highlands, Canyon Creek Point and Canyon Ridge Villas.

If one of those is your address and a pipe, drain or water heater is giving you trouble, call the number on this page and describe it. Ask about availability, timing and price on the call.

What counts as Canyon Creek

Several associations share the Canyon Creek name, and the City lists their locations a little differently. That is why this page names each one instead of drawing a single boundary.

The area sits in west Lenexa around Canyon Creek Boulevard, 99th Street and Prairie Star Parkway. Canyon Farms Golf Club, an 18-hole semi-private course, is listed in a golf directory as designed in 2007, which points to development in the 2000s. That is an indication of era, not a statement about any one house.

Basements, sump pumps and what the City requires

The City of Lenexa asks that plans for new homes show a sump pump and an electrical receptacle for it, and the City recommends a battery backup pump. That is a statement about new construction, so an older house may or may not have one.

A sump discharge pipe that ends within 4 feet of a property line does not meet the City code. Our sump pump installation and repair page explains what to check. If the pit stays wet after rain, see basement waterproofing for the next step.

HOA rules and villa communities

Some associations, including villa communities, handle exterior or shared maintenance. Before you call about anything outside the walls, ask your association what it maintains and what needs approval.

Inside your home, plumbing work is generally your responsibility. If you are not sure where the line sits, call us with your address and we will say what we can look at. We do not guess association rules.

Water heaters, hardness and fixtures

Water heaters fail at any age, and the choice between a part and a new tank depends on the unit. See water heater repair and water heater replacement.

We do not guess your water hardness. WaterOne publishes an annual water quality report, and a simple tap test also helps. If you want to treat the water, the water softener and filtration page explains the options.

Water, sewer and permits

WaterOne supplies drinking water and Johnson County Wastewater runs the public sewer at 913-715-8600. For permits, Lenexa Community Development at 913-477-7725 can say whether your project needs one under the 2018 International Plumbing Code (IPC).
