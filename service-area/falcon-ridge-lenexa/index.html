Plumber in Falcon Ridge, Lenexa, KS

Falcon Ridge is a group of neighborhoods in west Lenexa that grew up around a public golf course. The City's HOA list names seven Falcon Ridge and Falcon Valley associations.

If you live in one of them and need help with a leak, a drain or a water heater, call the number on this page and describe the problem. Ask about availability, timing and price on the call.

Where Falcon Ridge is

The City lists the main Falcon Ridge association at Prairie Star Parkway and Woodland Drive. Falcon Valley, next door, is listed at Prairie Star Parkway and Woodland Road, with more associations nearby.

Falcon Ridge Golf Course is a public 18-hole course listed in a golf directory as designed by Craig Schreiner in 1997. It gives the area its name and a rough sense of when it was laid out. As with any subdivision, building dates vary from house to house.

Outdoor faucets, winter and shutoff valves

Homes near a golf course often have a lot of exterior plumbing, such as outdoor faucets and irrigation connections. Kansas winters can freeze unprotected lines, so know where your main shutoff is before cold weather arrives.

If a pipe has burst, close the main valve and call. Our burst pipe repair page lists what to do while you wait. For a slow, hidden leak, leak detection finds the source without tearing out walls.

Drains, toilets and fixtures

A slow tub, a gurgling floor drain or a toilet that will not clear usually starts in one line. Our drain cleaning page explains how we clear it, and the toilet repair page covers running and leaking toilets.

If a drain backs up again within weeks, a camera pass shows whether the problem is in your line or in the public sewer. See sewer inspection for how that works.

Before you call, note which fixtures are affected and whether the problem started after heavy rain, a freeze or a recent repair. That detail tells us whether to think about one fixture trap or the main line.

Water, sewer and permits

Drinking water here comes from WaterOne. The public sewer belongs to Johnson County Wastewater, which takes calls at 913-715-8600 when a blockage is on its main rather than on your lateral.

Before a water heater swap or a sewer repair, ask Lenexa Community Development at 913-477-7725 whether a permit applies under the 2018 International Plumbing Code (IPC). Three of the associations above are villa communities, so also ask yours what it maintains outside the walls.
