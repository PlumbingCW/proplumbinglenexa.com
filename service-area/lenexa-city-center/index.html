What Lenexa City Center is

The City of Lenexa describes City Center as a mixed-use, walkable neighborhood at the geographical center of the city, and says it is still under construction. It is a planned district, not a traditional subdivision of houses.

The developer, Copaken Brooks, calls it a 69-acre mixed-use development in the southwest quadrant of I-435 and 87th Street Parkway. Its list of current components includes The District, Renner 89, The Domain Apartments, Lifetime Fitness and the Kiewit Headquarters Campus.

The developer says the campus includes Lenexa's civic campus, with a recreation center, a new city hall, a Park University campus and a public market. Its full build-out plan calls for 375 residential units and nearly 1 million square feet of office and retail space.

Who to call first: tenant, owner or plumber

In an apartment or leased shop, the owner or property manager usually controls shared pipes, water heaters and drains. Report the problem to them first and ask who they use. Lease terms differ, so we do not guess who pays.

If you own a unit, or if the owner has asked you to arrange a repair, call us and describe the problem. We will say whether it sounds like a fixture problem in your space or a shared line that the building owner needs to approve.

Commercial plumbing needs in a mixed-use district

Shops, restaurants and offices ask more of a plumbing system than a house does. Kitchens send grease down the line, and restrooms see heavy daily use. Those spaces are covered by our commercial plumbing page.

If a kitchen line keeps slowing down, a full-pipe cleaning can help, as explained on the hydro jetting page. We keep residential and commercial work separate so that you get the right tools for the job.

Water, sewer and permits at City Center

WaterOne supplies drinking water in Lenexa, and Johnson County Wastewater runs the public sewer at 913-715-8600. In a building with shared lines, the owner or manager is usually the one to report public-main problems.

Lenexa has adopted the 2018 International Plumbing Code (IPC). Lenexa Community Development at 913-477-7725 can say whether a project needs a permit. Commercial work in a leased space often needs the landlord's approval too.
