Hydro Jetting Cast Iron Pipe in Lenexa, KS: Risks and Fit

By the Lenexa Pro Plumbing team · Published November 14, 2026.

Hydro jetting can clean cast iron drain pipe very effectively, because it scours scale, grease and roots from the pipe wall. It is also not right for every old line, since a badly corroded or cracked cast iron pipe can be damaged by high pressure.

The safe approach is to look first with a camera, then choose the pressure and nozzle for what the camera shows. This guide explains why.

Why cast iron clogs the way it does

Cast iron drain pipe was common in homes for decades. Over time the inside of the pipe develops rough scale, called tuberculation, and grease and paper catch on it. The pipe narrows, and every clog builds on the last.

A cable machine often clears the clog but only punches a hole through it. The scale and grease stay on the pipe wall, so the line clogs again.

Hydro jetting washes the whole circumference with a high pressure stream and restores flow far better than a cable alone.

How a careful jetting visit works on old pipe

Camera first

We run a camera to see scale, cracks, offsets and the condition of the pipe wall.

Choose pressure and nozzle

We pick a setting and tip suited to scale or grease and to the pipe condition.

Jet in passes

We work from the cleanout toward the main in controlled passes.

Camera again

A post-clean pass confirms the pipe is clear and shows any problems the scale was hiding.

Risks to ask about before anyone jets old pipe

A good contractor answers these without hesitating.

  • Thin or cracked pipe wallScale can be holding a weak pipe together, and removing it exposes leaks.
  • Offset joints or sagsHigh pressure can find and enlarge a weak joint.
  • Pressure settingThe pressure should match the pipe and the clog, not simply be set high.
  • Clean-out accessPoor access forces a worse route and a harder job.
  • Honest reportingYou should see what the camera found, good and bad.

When jetting reveals a hidden problem

Sometimes a clean line shows a crack or a leak the scale had plugged. That is not the jetting causing the damage. It is the camera finally seeing the real condition. A leak found this way would have failed anyway, and you now have a chance to plan a repair.

A good contractor explains this before the work starts, so you are not surprised by a finding afterward. If a repair is needed, a spot fix or a lining is often more practical than a full replacement.

We show you the footage and explain each option, whether it is a cleaning schedule, a repair or a replacement.

Lenexa notes: older homes and the county sewer

Lenexa was incorporated in 1907 and has grown steadily, so house ages vary. Older houses are more likely to have cast iron drain lines, while newer houses typically use plastic pipe. A camera shows which you have.

The public sewer belongs to Johnson County Wastewater, reachable at 913-715-8600. Your lateral from the house to the public main is generally your responsibility, and a camera pass shows where a defect sits. Jetting a cast iron lateral is routine work when the camera shows the pipe can take it, and any repair that follows is governed by the 2018 International Plumbing Code (IPC) that Lenexa has adopted.

How we judge whether the pipe can take jetting

The camera tells us three things. How thick the scale is, whether the pipe wall shows cracks or holes and whether the joints are tight. A pipe with heavy but even scale and tight joints is a good candidate. A pipe with flaking metal, holes or a sag is not.

If the pipe is borderline, we use lower pressure and a gentler nozzle, or we choose cable cutting first and recheck. Cleaning in stages avoids shocking a weak pipe.

We would rather tell you a line needs repair than clean it and hope.

What to expect after cleaning cast iron

Water should drain faster, and the gurgling and slow tubs often stop. A cleaned line does not make a damaged line new, so keep an eye on any signs of trouble.

Avoid pouring grease into the drain and keep wipes out of the toilet. A pipe cleaned by jetting stays clean longer if the waste going into it is gentler.

If a line clogs again within a few months, ask about a camera pass to look for a belly or a damaged section.
