Sewer Camera Inspection in Lenexa, KS

A sewer camera shows what is inside the pipe, so repairs address the real problem. We run camera inspections in Lenexa, KS for repeat clogs, pre-purchase checks, line locating and after-cleaning verification.

What a sewer camera inspection actually shows

A waterproof camera on a flexible rod travels through the cleanout or a pulled toilet, sending live video to a screen. We see the pipe wall, the joints, any standing water, roots and the point where your line meets the public main.

The camera head also carries a transmitter, so we can trace the line from above ground and mark its depth. That avoids guesswork before any dig.

You watch the footage with us, and we explain every finding. If something looks wrong, we show you the frame instead of describing it.

How an inspection visit runs

Find the access

We locate the cleanout, or pull a toilet if there is none.

Clean if needed

A heavily blocked line is cleared first so the camera can pass.

Run the camera

We record the full run to the main and note distances to each defect.

Review together

We explain what we saw and what, if anything, to do next.

When a camera inspection is worth the visit

An inspection is not for every home. It pays for itself in these situations.

  • Repeat clogsIf cleaning does not hold, the camera shows why.
  • Buying an older homeA pre-purchase scan reveals a problem before closing and gives you negotiating information.
  • Slow drains throughout the houseThe main line may be narrowing, and the camera shows by how much.
  • Before a remodel or landscape projectKnowing where the line runs avoids a cut pipe.
  • After a repair or cleaningA post-work pass confirms the line is clear and sound.

Johnson County context for older and newer homes

Lenexa was incorporated in 1907, and its housing spans from older central subdivisions to new construction on the west side. Johnson County Wastewater serves the public sewer, and homeowners are generally responsible for the line from the house to the public main.

The county also runs the voluntary JoCo Inflow program, which has inspected more than 55,000 properties since 1983. If your camera shows a floor drain or sump pump tied to the sewer, that is a connection the program is meant to find, and it can be reached at 913-715-8550.

A camera finding that points to a hydro clean is a good time to read about hydro jetting, which clears scale and grease. Any repair that follows is done to the 2018 International Plumbing Code (IPC) that Lenexa has adopted.
