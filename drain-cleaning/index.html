When a clogged drain is an emergency

Most single-fixture clogs can wait until morning. A few cannot. If wastewater is rising in a basement floor drain, if two or more fixtures back up together, or if a toilet bubbles when the washer drains, the main line is blocked and the next load of water has nowhere to go.

That situation is worth a same-day call because sewage in a finished basement ruins flooring and drywall quickly. We answer drain emergencies 24/7 in Lenexa and send a crew with cable machines and a jetter on the truck.

Water from a backed-up drain can also carry bacteria, so keep children and pets out of the wet area. Do not run the dishwasher or washing machine, and do not pour chemical drain opener into a line that is already full, because the chemical sits in standing water and can splash on whoever works on it next.

What homeowners notice before a drain fails

Drains rarely fail without warning. These are the signs we hear about most, and each one points to a different depth of clog.

  • Water that drains slowly in every sinkSlow flow across the house usually means the main line is narrowing, not a single trap.
  • Gurgling from a toilet or floor drainAir pushed back up the line often shows a blockage downstream that is only partly open.
  • A tub that fills when the washer runsThe tub is the lowest open fixture on many Lenexa basement bathrooms, so it shows a main line backup first.
  • Sewer smell near the cleanout or floor drainA dry trap or a partial backup lets sewer gas into the room before water appears.
  • A clog that returns every few weeksRepeat clogs mean something is holding material in the pipe, such as roots, a belly, or grease coating.

Kitchen sink clogs: grease, food and the garbage disposal

Kitchen lines clog differently from bathroom lines. Cooking fat cools inside the pipe, narrows it, and catches rice, coffee grounds and pasta. By the time the sink stops, the pipe is often half closed for several feet.

We start with the trap and the line to the wall, then run a cable through the branch. If the clog sits past the wall tee, we cable the stack or jet the line. When the disposal is jammed or leaking as well, our garbage disposal repair service handles it on the same visit.

A short tip that saves a call: pour used cooking fat into a can and throw it away, and run hot water for a moment after the disposal rather than before it.

Your private line, the public sewer and who owns what in Lenexa

Wastewater service in Lenexa is provided by Johnson County Wastewater, which asks homeowners with floor drain problems to contact a plumber or the utility at 913-715-8600. The pipe from your house to the public main is generally the homeowner's responsibility, so a clog between the cleanout and the street is usually yours to clear.

A good first diagnostic step is the cleanout. If the cleanout cap is off or buried, we find it. If water stands in the cleanout, the blockage is downstream and our sewer line cleaning crew can cable or jet from that point toward the main.

Johnson County also runs the voluntary JoCo Inflow program, which has inspected more than 55,000 properties since 1983 and removed about 17,000 stormwater connections that overload the sewer in heavy rain. If rain, not a clog, triggers your basement drain backups, ask about it at 913-715-8550.

Drain line leaks and slow drains that are really damaged pipes

A drain that clogs again right after cleaning may be leaking or sagging. A belly collects solids, and a cracked joint lets soil and roots in. We confirm with a camera before recommending a repair, and the findings guide whether you need a sewer line repair or only regular maintenance.
