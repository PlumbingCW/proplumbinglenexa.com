Emergency Hydro Jetting in Lenexa, KS

When a cable only punches a hole through a clog, the problem returns in weeks. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the full pipe wall, and it is the fix for greasy, scaled or root-filled lines in Lenexa, KS.

If your main line is backing up right now, stop using water and call. Our line is open 24/7 and jetters are on the truck.

Sewer backup: what to do first

Hydro jetting is often the answer when a main line fails again and again. If you are watching water rise right now, the order matters. Stop using water everywhere in the house, keep family members out of the wet area, and open a cleanout cap only if you know where the cleanout is and can do it safely.

Call us even if the water recedes on its own. A partly open main will fill again with the next load of laundry, and the first backup shows where the line is weakest.

We send a crew with cable machines and a jetter. A camera pass after cleaning confirms the pipe is intact before we recommend any further work.

When jetting beats a cable

A cable opens a channel. Jetting cleans the whole pipe. These patterns usually mean jetting is worth it.

  • Repeat clogs within monthsGrease and scale line the wall. A cable cuts through but leaves most of it behind.
  • A cable that stops or gets stuckA cable that catches at a certain distance often meets a root mass or heavy scale that jetting can cut.
  • Root intrusion at jointsA jetter with a root-cutting nozzle can clear fine root hairs a cable misses.
  • Restaurant-style grease loadsHeavy cooking and large households put more fat in the line.
  • Old cast iron drainsRough interior scale slows flow, and jetting restores it when the pipe is still sound.

Older Lenexa neighborhoods and the lines under them

Lenexa has grown fast since its 1970 population of 5,242, and the city now covers 34.5 square miles. Subdivisions such as Whispering Hills and Century Estates date to the 1970s and 1980s according to listing records, and the sewer laterals under homes of that age are old enough that scale and roots are common.

Newer areas such as Canyon Creek and Bristol Ridge have newer pipe, where problems more often come from grease or construction debris than from age. Either way, the camera shows which situation you have.

If the camera finds a crack, offset joint or belly, jetting alone will not fix it, and our sewer line repair crew can explain the repair options.
