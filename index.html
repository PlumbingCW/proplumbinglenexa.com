What Lenexa homes and utilities mean for your plumbing

Lenexa was platted in 1869 by civil engineer Octave Chanute and incorporated in 1907. It now covers 34.5 square miles, and the housing stock runs from older central subdivisions to new homes near the K-7 and Prairie Star Parkway growth area. That mix matters, because a 1970s split-level and a 2010s home fail in different ways.

Wastewater is handled by Johnson County Wastewater, and the pipe from your house to the public main is generally your responsibility. A backup that starts in your own line is a job for a plumber, while a blockage in the public main belongs to the utility at 913-715-8600. Our drain cleaning and sewer line cleaning crews can tell which side of the property line the problem sits on.

Water comes from WaterOne, which draws from the Kansas and Missouri rivers and publishes an annual water quality report. WaterOne also says it has no record of lead service lines in its system, and its Service Line Lookup Tool shows the material of the line at your address.

Water emergency in Lenexa? Do these four things first

  1. Close the main water valve, usually where the line enters the basement or crawl space.
  2. Switch off power to wet areas if you can reach the panel without standing in water.
  3. For a gas smell, leave and call 911 and your gas utility before calling a plumber.
  4. Call (855) 575-2890. Our line is open 24/7 for burst pipes, backups and failed heaters.

Basements, sump pumps and heavy rain

Lenexa requires egress in finished and unfinished basements, so many homes have usable lower levels. The City says new homes must show a sump pump and an electrical receptacle in the plans, and it recommends a battery backup pump. If your sump discharge sits within 4 feet of a property line, it does not meet the City code.

When water arrives, a failed sump is one of several causes. A sump pump installation or repair visit checks the pump, the discharge and the float. Persistent seepage points toward basement waterproofing, drainage and grading fixes. And when the water is coming from inside the walls rather than outside, leak detection finds it.

Water heaters, the most common call after dark

A leaking tank rarely waits for business hours. If the unit is leaking from the body, water heater replacement is the realistic path, and if only a part has failed, water heater repair may be enough. Planned work, such as moving a heater or choosing a tankless water heater, is easier to price and schedule.

Lenexa has adopted the 2018 International Plumbing Code (IPC), the 2018 International Fuel Gas Code and the 2017 National Electrical Code (NEC), and our installs follow those standards. Permit questions go to Lenexa Community Development at 913-477-7725.
