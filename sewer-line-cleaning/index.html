Main sewer line cleaning starts at the cleanout

The cleanout is a capped pipe, often in a basement, crawl space or yard, that gives direct access to the main line. We open it, and what we see tells us a lot. Water standing in the cleanout means the blockage is downstream. A dry cleanout means the problem is upstream, in a branch line.

From the cleanout we run a cable machine toward the street. If it finds roots, scale or a mass of paper and grease, we choose a cutter head or a jetter. We clean the pipe, then pull the cable back and check the flow.

If there is no cleanout, we pull a toilet or use a roof vent. We recommend installing a cleanout afterward because it saves time and money on every later visit.

Sewage in the basement: protect the house while we come

These steps limit contamination.

  1. Stop all water use, including washing machines and dishwashers.
  2. Do not use chemical drain cleaner. It may be in the line when we work and is dangerous on skin.
  3. Keep people and pets out of the wet area. Sewage water carries bacteria.
  4. Move belongings off the floor if you can do it without entering standing sewage.
  5. Photograph the backup for your insurer and call us.

What a blocked main line looks like

The pattern across the house reveals the problem.

  • The lowest drain overflows firstA basement floor drain or tub is usually the first to show a main line backup.
  • Toilets bubble when a fixture drainsAir trapped in the line is pushed to the nearest vent and trap.
  • Several fixtures slow togetherOne blocked line affects every fixture that feeds into it.
  • Gurgling sounds in the wallsPartial blockage lets air escape unevenly.
  • Sewer smell indoorsGas can reach living areas when traps empty or the line backs up.

Which side of the property line is the clog on?

In Lenexa, Johnson County Wastewater serves the public sewer. A floor drain backup should be addressed to a plumber or to the utility at 913-715-8600, and a camera pass tells us which side of the property line the blockage is on.

If the blockage is past your property line, we document it with a video and you can call the utility. If it is on your lateral, we clear it. Either way you know which party is responsible.

A line that must be cleaned several times a year is a candidate for a camera inspection to find the cause. Lenexa has adopted the 2018 International Plumbing Code (IPC), and any repair that follows is done to it.
